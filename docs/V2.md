# AEGIS v2

**Result update:** the user subsequently reported **4.50**, the best known
practice score so far. This commit remains the scored reference for v6.

The user's first reported practice result was **3.75**, with **74th place** in
the supplied leaderboard screenshot. V2 subsequently improved that result to
4.50. A top-ten finish cannot be established by local tests.

## Concrete fixes

- A ready laser could take over the shared aim at close range even when it
  would not fire, leaving the gun unable to fire at a fast moving target. The
  gun now keeps its projectile intercept while the laser charges. The laser
  receives the shared aim when it is ready to release.
- Independent lightning fire now uses its own yaw-cone condition. It does not
  require matching the gun's projectile lead or pitch.
- Gun alignment is checked against the actual torso with a tighter angular
  tolerance. A blocked center shot can use an exposed upper-body aim point.
- Recovery persists until approximately 88% health instead of resuming a
  fight just above half health. During safe recovery, outgoing damage stops
  so regeneration can begin.
- Enemies tracked behind solid cover do not count as exposed close threats.
- Combat steering respects a planned route when the direct approach is
  blocked. A short safe step is allowed when long look-ahead rejects every
  direction at a tight corner. Failed movement commands still trigger escape.

## Policy changes

The bot favors magazine and damage upgrades more strongly, keeps more distance
with its gun or laser, avoids starting a laser charge inside an enemy's likely
lightning reach, shrinks its preferred combat distance with the zone, and
actively farms an isolated drone at a safe distance. Collecting more cubes
after the damage/health stat cap has lower priority.

## Validation and limits

The tactical regression suite contains 24 tests. New regression tests reproduced
the close-range firing and premature-recovery failures on v1 before the fixes.
Local recorded-stream conformance and synthetic stress timings are checked
before publication. The GitHub workflow builds linux/amd64 and runs the exact
Docker sandbox conformance command on the published commit.

A focused, simplified moving-target probe also reproduced the close-range
firing failure. This probe does not simulate opponents, the map, spread, or
placement, and does not predict leaderboard performance.
