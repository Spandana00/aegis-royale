below v2's **4.50**. The local
reload exposure improvement did not translate into a higher official score.

## Official results and target

The user reported v1 at 3.75, v2 at **4.50**, v3 at 3.96, and v4 at 4.40.
V2 is still the best scored version. The target of 5.30 requires 0.80 more
placement points per match, approximately 39 extra placement points over a
48-match run. V5 is unscored. No local test establishes a score or rank.

Practice house bots cooperate and do not eliminate each other. Avoiding all
combat therefore cannot rely on other bots dying. This experiment aims to
reduce damage during periods when the gun cannot return fire, while preserving
loaded firing opportunities.

## Change from v2

V2 uses its ordinary fighting movement throughout a gun reload, even without
a usable secondary. V5 searches for nearby hard cover during that window.
The search checks the nearest three tracked mechs' muzzle positions against
three heights on the bot's scaled body, rejects unsafe zone destinations,
requires a reachable ground component, and checks room for the full body.
Trees do not count as hard cover.

The search is bounded and cached for half a second. If no suitable cover is
found, ordinary v2 movement continues. A ready secondary weapon keeps combat
active. Low-health recovery and urgent zone movement retain priority.

The bot reserves roughly half the reload for returning, records where the
reload maneuver began, and starts returning before the gun is loaded. It stops
the return early when the loaded gun has a target. This prevents a reload
maneuver from turning into indefinite hiding. Weapon aiming and firing,
ordinary fight selection, upgrades, loot, and v2's dodge weights are preserved.

## Map diagnostic and tradeoff

`check/reload_probe.py` compares v2 and v5 at the same sampled campus positions
near buildings, with an initially clear firing line from a stationary observer
85–140 m away. Position selection does not depend on the candidate finding
cover. Each pair uses acceleration-limited 20 Hz ground movement and 10 Hz
decisions, followed by two seconds to check when a loaded trigger can fire.

| Set | V2 exposed seconds | V5 exposed seconds | Reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 32 positions, seed 2421 | 76.05 | 71.60 | 5.85% |
| 32 fresh positions, seed 32917 | 74.05 | 70.50 | 4.79% |

No scenario in these sets had greater reload-window exposure. V5 had a loaded
firing opportunity at the first post-reload decision in all 64 scenarios.
Raw paired results are retained in `reload_development.json` and
`reload_validation.json`.

An earlier version traveled farther and reduced exposure by about 15–20%,
but did not reserve return time. The final candidate deliberately gives up
most of that local exposure gain to preserve the next loaded firing window.

This diagnostic is **not the organizer's simulation or a placement scorer**.
It approximates wall sliding and omits moving opponents, enemy decisions,
projectiles, damage, deaths, jumping, jets, loot, and changing zone phases.
Its first-trigger measurement is a firing opportunity, not a confirmed hit.
These limitations matter, especially if real opponents move around the cover.

Reproduce from a clone containing v2's submitted commit:

```sh
python3 -B check/reload_probe.py --seed 2421 --trials 32
python3 -B check/reload_probe.py --seed 32917 --trials 32
```

## Validation and next decision

The 37-test tactical suite includes reload cover, continuing secondary attacks,
returning before reload completion, immediate loaded combat, full-body cover,
and zone/healing priorities. The official recorded-stream protocol checker
answered all 300 observations in time. The single-core stress probe processed
600 observations without invalid actions or responses over 50 ms (p99 14.54 ms,
maximum 18.90 ms on this host). Navigation completed all 12 map routes.
GitHub Actions builds the pinned linux/amd64 image and runs the Docker sandbox
conformance check on the submitted commit.

Use the official 48-match score as the decision criterion. Preserve v2 commit
`5209875b7f6f512de87b0411b6c44258cd113fbb` and its 4.50 submission. Do not pick
v5 as the final entry unless its official result justifies doing so.
