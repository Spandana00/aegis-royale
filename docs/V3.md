## What the policy audit found

- V2 could close to lightning range at only 58% health with modest energy.
- Its healing state was tied to the current movement mode. A zone rotation
  could replace that mode and allow outgoing hits to reset regeneration.
- Low-health retreat took priority over finishing a safe, nearly complete
  supply drop, even though the drop grants full health and energy.
- It still pursued some cubes after their health and damage bonuses capped,
  while additional cubes continued increasing its target size.
- A fixed zone look-ahead did not reserve extra time for building detours.
- Retreat cover was sought at a single nearby distance, potentially missing
  a useful building slightly farther away.

## The v3 experiment

Healing is now a persistent state through zone movement. It starts sooner
after a large damage burst or multiple active threats and continues until
roughly 90% health. Quiet healing suppresses outgoing damage, and retreating
mechs can face a recently observed attacker to protect rear armor.

Closing for lightning requires greater health and energy reserves, or a weak
isolated target that can be finished quickly. Ordinary gun and laser combat
remain available; the bot does not try to win practice simply by hiding.

Safe healing-drop captures can take priority over retreat. The decision checks
capture time, zone safety, damage already received, and observed opponents'
possible arrival times. Unknown opponents remain a risk.

Loot beyond the stat cap has lower priority unless the next cube immediately
unlocks an upgrade. Loot paths that approach an enemy while health is depleted
are rejected. Zone rotations reserve a conservative estimate of travel time,
and cover searches consider both nearby and farther points using the enemy's
muzzle height and the mech's upper body.
